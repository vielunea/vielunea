ABOUT THE AUTHOR

A student. A writer. Someone who understands that some days are hard.

Hi, I'm Vielunea. I'm currently a second-year college student who writes self-help books for people who may be going through difficult times, feeling lost, or simply needing someone to remind them that they're not alone.

I started writing these books because I know what it is like to have a hard time and still have to keep moving. My hope is that every page feels like a quiet conversation — honest, gentle, and comforting without pretending that life is always easy.

Supporting Vielunea also supports my journey as a student-writer who is working hard to support herself. Even choosing one of my books can already mean a lot to me. Thank you for being here. ♡

— Vielunea
